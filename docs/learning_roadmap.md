Instead of:

“train AI to predict the stock market”

Think:

“build systems that help humans detect useful structure, regimes, anomalies, and probabilities.”

That mindset is MUCH more realistic and educational.

The market is extremely noisy. Even elite quant firms struggle to find durable edges. But learning how to:

structure data
test hypotheses
identify patterns
evaluate signals
avoid false correlations
combine human reasoning + AI

…is absolutely achievable and incredibly valuable.

🧠 What You Actually Need To Learn

There are really 5 separate domains here:

Domain	Purpose
Market Structure	understand what moves markets
Data Engineering	collect/store/process data
Statistics & Probability	distinguish signal from noise
Machine Learning	pattern detection
AI Agents & LLMs	reasoning and synthesis

Most beginners jump straight to AI and skip the foundations.

That creates “smart-looking nonsense systems.”
