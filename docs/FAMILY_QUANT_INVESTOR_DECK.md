**Title:** The "Safety Cage": Strict Human Control over the AI

- **Our Strict Human-Locked Safety Rules:**
  1. **Strict Budget Envelope:** The human owner locks the wallet (e.g., \$20,000 max). The AI physically cannot access more money.
  2. **No Borrowed Money (Zero Leverage):** We never borrow money to trade. Every trade is 100% paid for in cash.
  3. **No Short Selling:** We only buy high-quality companies; we never take risky bets against the market.
  4. **Single-Stock Cap:** No single stock can ever exceed \$5,000 (25% max), ensuring no single event can harm the portfolio.
  5. **Physical Emergency Red Button:** A single click instantly freezes all trading and locks all money in cash.
